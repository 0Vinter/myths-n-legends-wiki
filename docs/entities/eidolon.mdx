**Eidolon** is a malevolent spirit that drifts silently through the Umbral Wasteland, preying upon those who wander too far from safety. Unlike the restless dead that lash out in blind rage, Eidolons are cunning hunters that delight in terror. They stalk their victims from afar before vanishing into the void, only to reappear at the worst possible moment.

- **Health**: 45 HP
- **Damage**: 6.5 DP
- **Armor**: 0 AP
