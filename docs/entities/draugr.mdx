**The Draugr** is a frost-bound variant of the Zombie, adapted to survive in the coldest regions of the Overworld. It behaves similarly to its undead counterpart but can also emerge from the frozen landscape.

## Stats
- **Health**: 35 HP
- **Damage**: 4 DP
- **Armor**: 3 AP

---

## Unique Features
- **Zombie Variant**: The Draugr is a variant of the Zombie, sharing its basic behavior and characteristics.
- **Cold Adaptation**: The Draugr can naturally spawn in snowy biomes.
- Zombies that enter Powder Snow are transformed into Draugr.

---

## Location
- Can be found in **Crimson Forests**.
