**Omecuaime**is a fearsome creature of the swamps, capable of attacking its enemies from both close and long range. Its multiple heads act independently, making its attacks unpredictable.

## Stats
- **Health**: 120 HP
- **Damage**: 12 DP(Melee)/ 8 DP(Ranged)
- **Armor**: 4 AP

---

## Unique Features
- **Independent Heads**: Each head independently decides which type of attack to use, switching between melee and ranged attacks depending on the situation.
- **Rage Mode**: When the Omecuaime reaches 30% health, it lets out a powerful roar and enters Rage Mode. The appearance of its heads changes to visually warn players that it has entered its enraged state.
- While in Rage Mode, the Omecuaime:
- Takes reduced damage.
- Gains increased movement speed.
- Gains increased armor.
- Deals increased damage with both melee and ranged attacks.

---

## Location
- Can be found in **Swamps** and **Mangroves**.
