The **Jiangshi** is an undead creature that emerges during the Blood Moon, bringing a dangerous supernatural presence to the night. When pushed to the brink of death, it enters a powerful state of stasis to restore its health, making it difficult to defeat without the proper means.
## Stats
- **Health**: 25 HP
- **Damage**: 4 DP
- **Armor**: 2 AP

---

## Unique Features
- **Stasis**: When the Jiangshi reaches 10% health, it enters Stasis and begins regenerating its health.
- While in Stasis, the Jiangshi cannot be killed normally.
- Stasis can be interrupted by using the Spirit Suppressing Bell.
- The Jiangshi can also be prevented from entering Stasis while affected by Weakness.

- **Spirit Suppressing Bell**: The Spirit Suppressing Bell applies the Spirit Suppression effect, preventing undead mobs from attacking while the effect is active.
- Against the Jiangshi, the bell has an additional effect: it interrupts its Stasis and deals damage to it.
- This makes the bell an essential tool for defeating a Jiangshi that has entered Stasis.

- Always near a player.
