**The Chronicler's House** is a mysterious structure that appears in the Overworld when the Chronicler first arrives in the player's world. Serving as the Chronicler's home, it becomes the central location for accessing the Research System and progressing through the content of **Myths & Legends**.

## Overview
- Generates near the player's respawn point when the Chronicler is first activated.
- The Chronicler resides inside the house.
- Does not generate in the Umbral Wasteland.
- Provides access to the Chronicler's Research System.

---

## Features
- Contains the Chronicler, who serves as the main interface for Research Projects.
- Allows players to progress through Research Projects and unlock content from Myths & Legends.
- Research progress is tracked individually for each player.

---

## Loot
- Contains loot similar to certain houses found in Minecraft villages.

---

## Dangers
- None.

---

## Notes
- If the Chronicler dies, a countdown begins before another Chronicler appears at the house.
- The Chronicler's accumulated XP and progression are preserved after death..
