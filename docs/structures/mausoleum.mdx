The **Mausoleum** is a cursed location found scattered throughout the world in **Myths & Legends**. These eerie structures are home to undead threats and rare loot, offering both risk and reward for brave adventurers.

## Overview
- Generates naturally in Deserts, Badlands and Umbral Wastelands.
- Contains the [Altar of the Dead](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/items/altar_of_the_dead), a key component for summoning the [The Black Charro](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/entities/black_charro).

---

## Loot
- None.

---

## Dangers
- Used to summon the powerful boss **The Black Charro**, making it a high-risk area for rituals.

---

## Notes
- Essential location for progressing through boss encounters.
- Recommended to approach at night only if fully prepared for battle.
