**Spirit Suppression** is a status effect used against undead mobs. While under this effect, undead are completely immobilized and unable to perform any actions.

## Effect
- The affected entity is frozen in place.
- All movement and interaction are disabled.
- Stronger Undead Mobs recover faster.
