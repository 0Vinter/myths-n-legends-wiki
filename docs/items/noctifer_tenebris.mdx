A relic of shadow and silence, it channels the void into devastating orbs of pure darkness and allows its wielder to bind enemies with an Umbral Hand.

## Stats
- **Attack Damage:** 8.
- **Attack Speed:** 1.6.
- **Attack Range:** 3 blocks.

## Obtaining
- Its crafting recipe is unlocked through the **Research System**.

## Usage
- Weapon.

## Ability
- **[Right-Click]** to shoot an umbral orb.
- **[Shift + Right-Click]** while looking at a target to summon an Umbral Hand that binds the target in place, preventing it from moving.
- Cooldown: 8 seconds. Reduced to 3 seconds under the moonlight.
