Its hollow chime disrupts lingering spirits and weakens those bound to undeath.

## Stats
- None.

## Obtaining
- **Research System**.
- The Tainted Bell required for its recipe can be found in chests within Stronghold Libraries, Woodland Mansions, and Ancient Cities.

## Usage
- Tool.

## Ability
- **[Right-Click]** to stun nearby undead and increase the damage they take. **Stronger undead recover from the stun faster.**.
- **Cooldown:** 10 seconds.
